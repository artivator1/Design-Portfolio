03 About

Designer.
Creative thinker.
Builder.

I'm Ayomide Adegboro, a visual designer interested in the space between strategy, culture and design.

My work moves between brand identity, creative direction, digital design and visual communication. I like taking an idea that feels complicated and finding the visual language that makes it feel obvious.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria. Working across brands, people and ideas.
