THE PROJECT

Making the student journey feel clearer.

Scooler is an AI powered platform built around the different moments students encounter while navigating education, applications and their future.

The visual direction was designed to make intelligence feel approachable. Instead of leaning into the expected language of futuristic AI, the identity needed to feel human, youthful, confident and easy to understand.
